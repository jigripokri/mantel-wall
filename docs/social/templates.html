The tenth star
Fill the row and the whole wall stops.
A parent taps a star from the couch and says why. Nine seconds of celebration, once a week, across all 1920×1080.

The phone talks.
Tap a star, upload a photo, switch the layout, put the wall to sleep. Every change repaints the TV instantly over Realtime.

Every name lives in one file.
Fork it, edit one config, and it's your family on the wall. Sign-in emails deliberately live in the database, never in the bundle.
