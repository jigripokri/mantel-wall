What is Mantel?
Recommendation

1d. It's the only structure that makes the phone and the wall visibly the same object — the same stepping-stone drawing, the same type, the same restraint — while still being a plain scroll a tired parent can operate one-handed. 1e is more delightful and worth keeping as a possible "kid mode," but its single key leaves nowhere obvious for photos and chores to land. 1c is out: the tab bar is the one thing in the set that reads like an app rather than like Mantel.

Type & rhythm
36 / 1.1
Screen title — Newsreader 400 ("Alex's week", "The family wall…" runs 46).
30 / 1.15
Sheet title — Newsreader 400.
38 / 1
Star count — Newsreader 400, tabular-nums; "of 10 stars" at 21 Newsreader 300.
26 / 1
Primary button label — Newsreader 500 on gold.
19 / 1.2
List row title — Newsreader 400.
17 / 1.2
Reward chip label, secondary actions — Newsreader 400 / Instrument Sans 400.
13 / 1
Section label — Instrument Sans 600, .22em, uppercase, ink-dim. Eyebrow uses .3em.
Grid
390 wide; 24px page padding; cards inset 16px, 22–26px internal padding; 16px between cards; 10–12px between chips. No radii anywhere except circles and the phone shell — square corners are the Mantel tell.
Components
Star plate
Card, 104px drawing area. Ten fixed points, x = 3 + i·10.3%, y = 50 + 30·sin(i·0.8)% — identical formula to the wall's D5 so the two read as one artwork. Earned = ★ Newsreader 400 / 26px in star with text-shadow: 0 0 14px hsl(44 74% 56% / .5); unearned = 8px line-soft dot. Reward emoji + 60px hairline plate at the right end.
Add a star
Full-bleed inside the card, 92px tall, solid star, ink = surface. Press: scale .98, 90ms. Never disabled; at 10 of 10 it reads "Add a bonus star" and the plate goes gold.
Star landed
Full-screen overlay, ~1.6s, tap-to-dismiss. Radial gold wash at 16%, a 132px ★ scaling 0.6 → 1 with a 300ms spring, the ordinal in Newsreader 34, one line of plain-language distance, then ten dots where the new one is 12px and lit. No confetti, no sound by default.
Reward picker
Full-height sheet. 15 presets in three named groups (Out & about / At home / Little treats), two per row — emoji 22px + Newsreader 17 label, 44px minimum row height. Selected row: 1px star ring + card fill. "Make your own" is a dashed row pinned under the last group.
Custom reward
120px emoji well, a 6×3 emoji pad, and a Newsreader 30 title field underlined in star while focused. A live preview row sits above the keyboard so you see the chip exactly as the wall will show it.
Wall switch
Two full-width preview tiles that are miniature renderings of the actual layouts (not screenshots) with a one-line description; the live one carries the gold hairline and an "On the wall" label. Selecting pushes immediately — no Save.
Future homes
"Add a photo" and "Chores" already sit as a 50/50 pair of dimmed cards under the wall card; when they ship they light up and gain their own sheets. Nothing else moves.
Touch, motion, contrast

Every tappable row is ≥44px; the primary action is 92px and sits in the lower third. Motion is limited to three things: the button press, the star-landed overlay, and a 200ms crossfade between sheets. Nothing loops.

Dark: ink 14.6:1, ink-dim 6.8:1, ink-faint 3.6:1 (≥17px only), accent 6.9:1, surface-on-gold 11.3:1. Light: ink 12.9:1 on oat, ink-dim 6.2:1, moss accent 6.8:1, and gold is darkened to hsl(38 68% 46%) so cream-on-gold clears 4.6:1 — the one token that differs between tones by more than lightness.
