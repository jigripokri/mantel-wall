D5 locked geometry
Footprint
620 × 260 inside the side panel (no frame, no radius, no shadow — the panel's own background shows through). In context it occupies 200px under the schedule's section heading.
Stone field
400 × 132, inset 44px from the panel's left padding edge, vertically centred. Ten points, x = 3 + i·10.3 (% of width), y = 50 + 30·sin(i·0.8) (% of height), i = 0…9. Positions are constant — the same ten places every week.
Earned stone
★ glyph, Newsreader 400, 32px, star gold, centred on its point (−50% both axes), text-shadow: 0 0 18px hsl(44 74% 56% / 0.5).
Unearned stone
10px circle, star-quiet (day hsl(34 20% 82%) / evening hsl(30 12% 30%)). No glyph, no border, no glow.
Reward
Right-aligned, vertically centred, 40px inset: emoji at 48px, then a 14px gap, then an 84px plate rule. Plate is 1px line and the emoji sits at 0.7 opacity (0.4 at zero stars).
Earned state
Plate thickens to 2px and turns star gold; emoji goes to full opacity with text-shadow: 0 0 30px hsl(44 74% 56% / 0.45). Nothing else changes — no copy, no badge, no layout shift.
Goal ≠ 10
Recompute x as 3 + i·(93/(goal−1)) and keep the same sine on y; the field absorbs 5–14 stones without touching anything else.
Idle polish
Optional: the most recently earned stone breathes its glow 0.35 → 0.6 over 6s, ease-in-out infinite alternate. State never depends on it.

Portable spec

Recommendation: R1, the constellation pane. It is the only option that is genuinely beautiful at zero stars — an empty night sky, not an empty container — and the completing shape gives a five-year-old something to watch grow without a single bar or percentage. R2 is the warmer, more literal fallback if the family wants the tally countable at a distance.

R1 geometry
Pane
620 × 340 outer; 34px padding; inner pane 1px pane-frame, no radius, no shadow. Vignette: radial-gradient(120% 120% at 50% 0%, rgb(0 0 0/0.05), transparent 70%) in day; the same in white at 4% for evening.
Star field
492 × 150 area, inset 30px left / 34px top. Ten fixed points at % of that box: (6,62) (16,34) (27,58) (38,28) (47,66) (57,38) (66,70) (76,32) (86,56) (95,24).
Earned star
★ glyph, Newsreader 400, 34px, color star, centered on its point (translate −50%), text-shadow: 0 0 18px hsl(44 74% 56% / 0.5).
Unearned point
10px circle, star-quiet, no glyph, no border.
Hairlines
1px div per consecutive pair, transform-origin: 0 50%, rotated to the angle between points. Drawn in star-line when both ends are earned, otherwise line.
Tally
Bottom-left, 30px inset: count in Newsreader 400 / 44px ink, tabular-nums; “of 10 stars” Newsreader 300 / 28px ink-dim.
Reward foil
78px circle, 1px ring, fill = 6% ink (day) / 8% cream (evening), emoji at 40px. On full: ring becomes star, fill 12% gold, box-shadow: 0 0 30px hsl(44 74% 56% / 0.35), and the reward name sets to its left in Instrument Sans 600 / 26px / .18em caps, accent.
Column fit
In the 620px panel the pane is 250px tall (star field 472 × 118, foil 74px, tally 40px) — see the in-context panels above. Nothing below 26px in any state.
Idle polish
Optional: the most recently earned star pulses its glow between 0.35 and 0.6 opacity over 6s, ease-in-out, infinite alternate. Meaning never depends on it.
Contrast

Gold on panel: 2.4:1 day / 6.1:1 evening — used only as a 34px glyph and never as text, so it clears the 3:1 graphical floor in evening and is carried in day by the ★ shape plus the Newsreader tally, which runs ink on panel at ≈ 12:1. Dim ink is 6.2:1 day / 6.6:1 evening. Accent (moss) is 6.8:1 day / 7.1:1 evening. Unearned points sit at ≈ 1.2:1 on purpose — they are a whisper, and the count is always stated in type.
